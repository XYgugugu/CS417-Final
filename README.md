## Overview

You are Crazy Dave — Crazetopher David Blazing III — living peacefully with plants on your lawn. But suddenly, **there are zombies on your lawn!** Manage resources, plant defensive flora, and interact with the environment to stop your lovely “neighbors” from eating your brain.

This project is a playful VR tower defense game inspired by *Plants vs. Zombies* by PopCap Games. Instead of only using a flat control panel, players can step into the garden, interact with plants and tools, and manage the battlefield directly.
